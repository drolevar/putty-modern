PuTTY
-----

PuTTY Modern includes PuTTY, by Simon Tatham and the PuTTY contributors: PuTTY's core,
unmodified (the SSH, Telnet, Rlogin, raw and serial connections, the terminal emulator, the
settings and PuTTY's own storage), and keyboard and drawing code adapted from PuTTY for Windows.
PuTTY is used under its own licence, which follows word for word. PuTTY Modern is an independent
project: it is not made or endorsed by Simon Tatham or the PuTTY team. The official PuTTY is at
https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/putty/

PuTTY Modern shares saved sessions and host keys with PuTTY (the registry key
HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\SimonTatham\PuTTY).
